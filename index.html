Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful?

No. Dental implant surgery is performed under local anaesthesia, so you feel no pain during the procedure. Mild discomfort for 2-3 days after is normal and managed with standard painkillers.

How long does laser whitening last?

Results typically last 1-2 years with proper oral hygiene. Avoiding staining foods/drinks like tea, coffee, and red wine helps maintain whiteness longer.

Is root canal treatment done in one sitting?

Yes! We use advanced rotary endodontic systems allowing most root canals to be completed in a single visit, saving you time while ensuring excellent results.

What age is suitable for braces?

There's no age limit! Braces work for children, teenagers, and adults. Invisalign is particularly popular among adults as it's nearly invisible and removable.

Do you offer EMI for expensive treatments?

Yes, we offer easy EMI options through leading banks and finance partners. Ask our team during consultation for details on 0% interest plans.

How sterile is the clinic?

We follow strict ISO 9001:2015 sterilisation protocols. All instruments are autoclaved, and we use disposable materials wherever possible. Your safety is our highest priority.
